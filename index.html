Built around what a school actually does

Not a generic form-builder adapted for education — every part of this platform is built around how admissions, classrooms, and fees really work.

📋

Admissions

Applicants apply online, sit a timed entrance exam matched to the exact class they applied for, and the school decides when ready.

📚

Classrooms

Attendance and continuous assessment recorded once — visible to the student and parent immediately, not just at report card time.

🏆

Report cards

Grades compute themselves, and promotion happens automatically at the start of a new session for students who earned it.

💳

Fees

A bill set once for a class applies automatically, even to a student admitted afterward — and parents can pay directly online.
